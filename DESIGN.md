# Log Note Design System

> Log Note should be quiet enough that recording feels effortless, and rigorous enough that years of records remain understandable to people and future AI systems.

## Product context

- **Product:** an account-owned, offline-capable personal recording app for linear notes and periodic daily records.
- **Primary task:** capture a record with the fewest possible decisions.
- **Secondary task:** maintain a dependable Domain → Category → Template structure.
- **Default language:** English, with complete Simplified Chinese UI support.

## Visual thesis

A precise recording instrument with the warmth of paper and the restraint of serious software. Typography, alignment and fine dividers express hierarchy; decoration does not.

- UI and body: Instrument Sans.
- Editorial headings: Instrument Serif.
- Time, order and numeric values: IBM Plex Mono with tabular numbers.
- Type scale: display `36–52px`, page/domain title `28px`, section title `22px`, category heading `18px`, body/content `16px`, UI label `14px`, metadata/count `12px`.
- Reading hierarchy follows content structure: page/date → domain → category → record or metric → value/supporting text. A child label or value must not visually overpower its parent category.
- In Category view, a domain and its first visible category may share one compact editorial chapter line while remaining separate semantic headings; later categories keep their own subordinate headings. Between adjacent domains, the final content-row rule plus `24–32px` section whitespace is sufficient—do not add a second domain-start rule.
- Read-only hierarchy uses size, weight, whitespace and progressive indentation. Decorative connector lines are not used inside record content; one mobile viewport-edge directory may anchor labels for the page sections that are actually visible, but it must not imply a new parent/child relationship or mutate structure.
- Spacing uses a 4px base. Prefer 4, 8, 12, 16, 24 and 32px.
- Proximity follows one repeated reading rhythm: paired details use `4px`, headings sit `8px` from their own content, sibling groups separate by `16px`, sections by `24px`, and major regions by `32px`. A child must always sit closer to its own heading than to the next sibling heading.
- Radius is functional: 5–7px for controls, `10px` only for a module whose surface is itself an interaction boundary, and larger radii only for floating actions or bottom sheets.
- Record labels share one compact geometry: `22px` height, `12px` type, `500` weight, `8px` horizontal inset and capsule radius. Account/domain/category scope uses a neutral surface; user-authored `#tags` use a low-saturation accent surface while preserving the `#` prefix.

### Archival journal surface

- The home journal may scope the shared paper system to warm archival paper (`#F4EFE5`), raised leaf paper (`#FBF7EE`), graphite ink (`#232018`) and deep writing blue (`#294F96`). These values belong to the journal surface and composer; they do not redefine settings, authentication, warnings or destructive states.
- On mobile, the existing right rail reads as a `56px` binding gutter: a slightly darker paper edge and shallow inset depth sit behind the established brush, directory and controls. Material comes from local paper texture, tone and edge depth—not stains, ornamental borders, heavy shadows or decorative antiquing.
- The active mobile domain may expose one separate `44px` local-insights action below its label. The label and action form one vertical column in the right rail with at least `4px` clearance; the label does not occupy the left reading surface, while the mark stays aligned to the spine and its domain control keeps the scroll-navigation role. The action uses a real transparent graphite asset and opens a secondary domain-focused page. It must not repeat for every domain, alter the upper rail order, add a recording step, or crowd the writing plane. Desktop may expose the same destination as a quiet secondary header action.
- Every Diary date owns one application-shell viewport Agent layer. The transparent graphite line spirit remains visible in `idle`, `scanning`, `reviewing`, and `complete`; at `320–700px` the button and artwork travel together only inside the safe spine segment between upper tools and lower actions, while wider screens use a still peek. Slow rail travel is separate from each six-frame local action: idle re-grips and lets its body follow, scanning stretches/retracts, reviewing keeps the hand-to-chin thinking pose while its gaze inspects the note, and complete coils/re-grips before settling. A populated idle date places one localized, two-line tap-to-analyze hint directly below the figure and moves it with the same traveler; empty dates, Calendar, scanning, reviewing, and complete hide the invitation. Plan, Search and Settings hide this Diary layer. The ordinary composer hides only the application-shell traveler and mounts one composer-local instance of the same Hero appearance as its optional content-improvement target, so two characters never overlap. Character assets contain no full-height spine stroke, so the existing rail is the only binding line. Focus, press, hidden document and reduced motion pause or remove travel. Empty-date activation shows only a temporary no-write margin note. Appearance selection stays an internal static/motion/profile asset contract and adds no settings, persistence, network access or backup fields.
- Every Plan date owns the existing illustrated Plan Agent presence near the add-plan action. With editable local plans it keeps the existing optional wake/review states; without editable local plans—including Google-only days—the artwork remains visible but non-interactive and pairs with one `12px` muted single-line invitation, Chinese exactly “编写计划后和我聊聊吧”. The passive state must not dispatch analysis, imply that Google events are editable, or compete with the add-plan control.
- Authored records remain `16px` Instrument Sans at full ink. Serif is reserved for the editorial title, `Record`, fixed-ledger labels and the composer title; mono remains time and numeric metadata. The material layer must never outrank or recolor raw note content.
- New-record creation keeps the lifted full-composer leaf available from the lower record stamp. Immediately after a populated idle ordinary-record surface, quick creation is one quiet aligned row rather than a standalone button: a `44px+` mono `HH:mm:ss` time target followed by a single-line input with only a faint functional surface. The time ticks while the input is unfocused, freezes on focus, and refreshes to the current second when activated; non-empty blur or Enter saves once, while empty blur/Escape do not write. This row must feel like part of the record stream, not a call-to-action card, and must not open a modal. Existing ordinary-record content still edits in-row; its leading time opens the narrow non-modal fine-tuning surface, which accepts legacy minute values and second-precision values. Read rows omit horizontal rules and retain `44px+` targets.
- In the ordinary editor, `More` is a real disclosure. Closed mode keeps the writing area primary;
  expanded mode keeps a `160px+` usable writing region on mobile but yields height to one compact
  metadata ledger, then a weakly separated attachment section and an existing-record danger footer.
  The writing focus is a slim blue margin mark rather than a hard rectangular field outline. At
  `320–350px`, date and time stack so native controls cannot clip; wider mobile widths keep the pair
  on one row. Presentation must not change field values, callbacks, exact text, attachment ownership,
  delete confirmation, or the one-action save path.
- The ordinary free-text composer may mount the existing local Hero inside the writing leaf as one
  `44px+` content-improvement target. The Hero watches without opening a panel; tapping non-empty
  text starts one request and reuses the writing area for the candidate. Review chrome is limited to
  one compact row for original/candidate viewing, explicit use, and cancellation. No chat, prompt,
  history, card, automatic rewrite, or automatic save is allowed. Empty text, cancellation, close,
  source changes, stale results, or failure keep the draft unchanged. Structured and periodic editors
  do not show this control.
- The record-view and workspace controls remain two separate one-action buttons. Diary keeps one
  localized `Category / 分类` button in the upper tools after Search and Settings: unpressed is the
  default timeline, while a raised paper surface, deep-blue ink, and `aria-pressed=true` identify the
  active grouped view without relying on color alone. The single Diary/Plan control remains a
  two-position paper rocker with both localized alternatives visible and one raised thumb, and lives
  in the lower quick dock in both Diary and Plan. Diary places a visible `Export today / 导出今日日记`
  action to the left of the blue record stamp; Plan hides those Diary actions and retains its own
  add-plan control. Visual and keyboard order match. Each rocker and action remains at least `44px`,
  shows keyboard focus, and moves immediately under reduced motion.

## Semantic type and proximity hierarchy / 字体与亲密性层级

| 语义层级 | 字体与字号 | 字重 | 颜色 | 行高 | 标题/信息 → 所属内容 | 同级相邻组 | 跨区 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 大标题（展示、页面、领域） | Instrument Serif；`28px`，首屏展示可用 `36–52px` | `400` | `--ink` | `1.2` | `12px` · `--space-cluster` | `32px` · `--space-region` | `32px` · `--space-region` |
| 中标题（区块） | Instrument Serif；`22px` | `400` | `--ink` | `1.2` | `8px` · `--space-related` | `24px` · `--space-section` | `32px` · `--space-region` |
| 小标题（分类、表单分组） | Instrument Sans；`18px` | `600` | `--ink` | `1.35` | `4–8px` · `--space-paired/related` | `16–20px` · `--space-group` + optional `--space-paired` | `24–32px` · `--space-section/region` |
| 正文与记录内容 | Instrument Sans；`16px` | `400`，强调为 `600` | `--ink` | `1.55` | 配对信息 `4px` | 段落或字段组 `12–16px` | `24px` · `--space-section` |
| 辅助信息（标签、计数、时间） | Instrument Sans `14px`；时间/数值用 IBM Plex Mono `12px` | `500–600` | `--muted`；弱提示可用 `--faint` | `1.45–1.55` | 紧贴所属信息 `4px` | `8–12px` | `16–24px` |

Typography contrast establishes importance; proximity establishes ownership. A child label, description, count or value must be visually weaker than its parent and closer to that parent than to the previous or next group. When a spacing value and this ownership rule conflict, preserve ownership first while keeping the 4px base and 44px interactive targets.

## Interaction rules

- Recording remains the shortest path and must not expose structure maintenance by default.
- Structure management uses explicit drag handles, not whole-row dragging.
- Pointer and keyboard dragging are never the only controls; every sortable row provides Move up, Move down and, where relevant, Move to… actions.
- Hover and control transitions use 140–180ms. Drag lift and drop use 180–240ms.
- Periodic records use rhythm/cadence and value styling; linear records use a compact timeline.
- Empty hierarchy groups are valid drop targets and show one quiet instructional line.
